1. EXECUTIVE COMMERCIAL THESIS & INCENTIVES
================================================================================
Mumbai nightlife venues systematically charge prohibitive stag entry fees 
(₹2,500–₹3,500+ per unaccompanied male), while couples enter with zero or 
substantially discounted door charges.

NITE CREW solves this supply-demand asymmetry via platform-coordinated, 
verified pair and squad matching:

A. FOR MALE PARTICIPANTS — THE INCENTIVE: 60%+ COST SAVINGS
   - Standard Venue Stag Reference Fee: ₹2,500–₹3,000+
   - NITE CREW Platform Entry Offer: Approximately ₹999 / ₹1,000 per person
   - Value Proposition: Guaranteed pairing at the door saves ₹1,500–₹2,000+ 
     while eliminating the awkwardness of being turned away at the velvet rope.
   - Optional: "Enhanced Entry Assistance" / confirmed gate check-in fee.

B. FOR FEMALE PARTICIPANTS — THE INCENTIVE: REWARDS, RECOGNITION & SAFETY
   - Motivation: Promotional benefits, zero stag hassles, and unilateral review authority.
   - Platform Reward: Selected events offer eligible participants digital reward points 
     or venue credits worth up to ₹700–₹800, redeemable against food and non-alcoholic 
     beverages at participating partner venues.
   - Safety Guarantee: Women retain 100% unilateral approval and veto authority before 
     any communication unlocks.

C. THE INDEPENDENCE PRINCIPLE — "YOUR NIGHT. YOUR CHOICE."
   - Crucial Rule: Once inside past the security gate, participants have complete 
     autonomy.
   - Participants may choose to continue socializing together, merge with the 4–6 
     person squad, or immediately go their own separate ways with zero social or 
     personal obligation.

7. SQUAD ARCHITECTURE — EQUAL-SPLIT (2M+2F / 3M+3F) COUPLE DOOR COMPLIANCE
================================================================================
How Squads of 4–6 Solve the Mumbai Club Door Policy:

Mumbai nightclubs strictly enforce couple-to-stag ratios at the gate. Groups of 4 or 6 
all-male attendees are universally rejected or charged exorbitant table minimums (₹25,000–₹50,000+).

NITE CREW's Squad Coupling Algorithm:
1. Balanced Ratio Target:
   - 4-Person Squads = 2 Male Members + 2 Female Members (1:1 Ratio)
   - 6-Person Squads = 3 Male Members + 3 Female Members (1:1 Ratio)
2. Subgroup Fusion:
   - If two male friends register together, the engine pairs them with two female friends 
     registering for the same event, merging them into a unified 4-member squad.
3. Gate Assembly:
   - The squad gathers at the Main Security Foyer at 10:15 PM.
   - The squad approaches the velvet rope together as balanced couples, allowing 
     smooth entry under standard couple guestlist protocols.
4. Flexible Table Split:
   - Once inside, squad members can split the table bill evenly, order individual 
     drinks, or explore the venue freely.
